## Implementation

- The three approved blue staging minions are now authoritative units. They advance without player input, acquire opposing minions/Guardian within the existing 560-unit perception radius, retain valid targets for at least 30 ticks, and share range, LOS, windup, armor, cooldown, HP and death rules with red minions. Hostile Towers are eligible targets; blocking hostile Walls are strategic breach targets. Friendly units and structures are never attack targets.
- Initial blue positions and artwork are preserved. The existing two-wave timing is reused: red waves at ticks 1 and 240; blue starts with three staged minions and receives three reinforcements at tick 240, with a 40-minion faction cap. Clear blue approach positions are `(500,552)`, `(500,648)`, `(552,504)`. These timings/positions are prototype balance assumptions because the specifications do not prescribe an allied wave schedule. Scripted waves have no Barracks cost or production queue.
- Deterministic A* uses the existing 40×24, 48-unit grid with four neighbors, static terrain/scenery footprints, dynamic structure occupancy and swept radius collision. Graph edges are sampled for clearance. Attack goals are reachable cells within the attacker's range and LOS; otherwise minions advance to approach points near the opposing illustrative base. Steering and separation remain collision checked; safe lateral lanes recenter before narrow passages.
- Navigation versions change on structure placement/removal. Intersecting routes invalidate immediately; unaffected routes can be retained until their scheduled refresh. Dead-unit caches are removed. Paths/failed searches refresh on a 30-tick deadline or changed target cell. At most two route requests run per tick, with rotating priority and a safe wait when budget is exhausted. Each request runs at most two searches, each visiting at most 960 nodes. Static radius grids are cached. No independent gameplay timers or Phaser dependency enters navigation.
- A strategic breach search assigns travel-time plus armor-adjusted HP/DPS costs to hostile destructibles. Permanent terrain and friendly structures remain impassable. Prefer a safe route unless breaching improves estimated time by more than 20%; use breaching when no safe route exists. A minion stops in attack range, damages the actual structure, and crosses only after collision is released by death. Plans reconsider after one second so another opened channel can supersede an obsolete breach. Existing windup/cooldown validation remains authoritative.
- Blue/red deaths clean units, sprites, HP bars, target references and minimap markers. Only red minion deaths grant the player's existing 15 Gold reward and increment kills; allied and structure deaths grant none. Repeated death/reward events are prevented by state removal and the existing economy ledger.
- The HUD observes state after all scenes update using Phaser's post-step event, fixing a one-frame resource/HP/minimap lag exposed at income boundaries. Lifecycle cleanup removes that listener. Approved art, HUD geometry, camera, joystick, Attack, skill previews, construction costs and fixed 30 Hz clock are preserved.

There is no match objective, win/loss flow, Phase 1D, Hero Skills, Artifacts, Campaign, Free Play, Barracks production, additional economy buildings or Android packaging.
