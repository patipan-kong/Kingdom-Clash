## Images (input `../../ref/master.png`)
### `windup.png`: the ram drawn BACK. The log slides to the right so that the iron head is pulled in under the front of the roof, its tip about at the front wheel's left edge; the chains swing a little back (the log hangs a little back and raised). Everything else identical.
### `contact.png`: the ram THRUST FORWARD at full extension. The log slides far to the left so that the iron head sits about 15 percent of the cart's width further left than the input's head (a long reach), the log nearly level, the chains swung forward and taut. Everything else identical.
### `recover.png`: the ram RETURNING. The head sits about 6 percent of the cart's width further left than the input's head, between the input's rest and the full thrust, the chains slightly swung. Everything else identical.
