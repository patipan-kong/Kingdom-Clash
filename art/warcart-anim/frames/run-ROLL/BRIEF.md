Rules: read and follow `../COMMON.md` (every rule there applies).

## Images (input `../../ref/master.png`; a wheel-turn series: the cart rolls to the LEFT, so each wheel turns counter-clockwise)
Eight-spoke wheels repeat every 45 degrees. Turn ONLY the spokes and the iron rim studs of ALL FOUR wheels (the two near wheels and the two far wheels partly hidden behind them) by the stated angle counter-clockwise about each wheel's hub; hubs, rim outlines, wheel positions and sizes do not move. Everything else is identical to the input, including the ram's position.
